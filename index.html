Our Core Innovations

Flagship Social Technologies

Discover STB's leading social welfare innovations designed to address deep-rooted issues and respond to priority needs.

Project Aruga

Project Aruga is a community based project that provides disability inclusive social protection to children with disabilities and their families.

Learn More

Pamilya sa Bagong Pilipinas

The Pamilya sa Bagong Pilipinas Program is a family development and welfare model that brings DSWD programs directly to families.

Learn More

ProtecTEEN

The ProtecTEEN program is a social welfare model fundamentally built upon a social contract with adolescent mothers and their families.

Learn More

Angel Pets

Angel Pets is a community based project that provides holistic animal assisted interventions to marginalized individuals and their care networks.

Learn More

Kalinga

Kalinga is a community based project that provides inclusive child protection interventions to at risk children and their vulnerable communities.

Learn More

Project SAFE

Project SAFE is a community based project that provides comprehensive online interventions to vulnerable children and their digital environments.

Learn More

Apo Ko

Apo Ko is a community based project that provides intergenerational structured mentoring to senior citizens and their early childhood companions.

Learn More

Peace Project

Peace Project is a community based project that provides trauma informed social protection to children in conflict and their recovering families.

Leadership

Director’s Corner

Director Helen Suzara leads the Social Technology Bureau with years of experience in creating new ways to help people in need. As the head of the department’s main office for research and growth, the Director works to turn fresh ideas into real world programs that protect Filipino families.

By using simple and modern tools, Director Helen makes sure that every program is easy to use and truly helpful for the public. Through this leadership, the Bureau continues to find better ways to serve children, families, and communities across the country.

Gender and Development (GAD)

The Social Technology Bureau integrates gender equality into all programs, ensuring its social technologies empower women and serve the diverse needs of the Filipinos and marginalized groups.
