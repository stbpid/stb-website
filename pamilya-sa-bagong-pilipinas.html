About the Program

What is PBP?

The Pamilya sa Bagong Pilipinas Program is a family development and welfare model under the Social Technology Bureau of DSWD. It matches Filipino families with government interventions tailored to their life stage and specific needs — bringing the right support to the right family at the right time.

The program establishes physical and online social welfare hubs while harmonizing SWD programs for consistency and efficiency, ensuring no family is left without the support they deserve.

Goals

Objectives

The Pamilya sa Bagong Pilipinas aims to:

  • Gather and maintain data on the vulnerable families using the PBP Registry;
  • Establish a physical or online social welfare hubs for community-level access;
  • Harmonize the Department's social welfare and development (SWD) programs for consistency and efficiency; and
  • Increase public awareness of the Department's programs.
Key Features

Program Components

PBP Social Registry

An online platform for registration, profiling, and service-matching for Filipino families, including a customized list of services.

PBP Hub

A digital and physical access point for beneficiaries to register, update information, and explore SWD programs through system-generated matches.

PBP Compendium

A ready reference for families to navigate programs, detailing eligibility requirements and referral procedures.

PBP Advocacy

Initiatives to raise public awareness of DSWD programs so more families are informed and equipped to avail of services.
