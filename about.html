Social Technology Bureau

As the innovation center of the Department of Social Welfare and Development, the Social Technology Bureau is dedicated to uplifting the lives of Filipinos. Our main duty is to design and develop fresh programs that answer the changing needs of our vulnerable citizens. We actively seek practical ways to ensure government support is compassionate and truly felt by those who need it most.

To ensure our initiatives truly work, we carefully test new ideas before launching them nationwide. We partner with local government units to run pilot projects directly within communities. This practical approach allows us to see how our solutions affect real families and adjust our programs based on actual feedback from the public.

Our ultimate vision is to build a strong and inclusive society where no Filipino is left behind. We blend modern strategies with the genuine spirit of care to create sustainable public services. By continuously improving how we protect our people, we empower communities and lay the foundation for a secure future for every family. This is our commitment at the Social Technology Bureau: Innovating Solutions with Compassion.

Vision

The Social Technology Bureau (STB) is the lead in the development of social welfare and development (SWD) programs and services for and with the poor, vulnerable, and disadvantaged Filipinos.

Mission

Formulate, enhance and promote social welfare models of intervention for the poor, vulnerable and disadvantaged Filipinos.

What We Are About

Social Technology Bureau Commitment

Responsive Development

Develop responsive, transformative, and sustainable SWD models of intervention that addresses the needs of the poor, vulnerable and disadvantaged groups.

Proactive Technology

Ensure proactive and improved social technology development process, thereby reducing turn-around time for the completion of models of intervention from conceptualization to pilot testing and evaluation.

Capacity Building

Provide continuing capacity building to its staff and intermediaries, and facilitate organizational development activities to ensure holistic, rights-based, evidence-based, and participatory technologies.

Accelerated Dissemination

Conduct accelerated and intensified social marketing of SWD models of intervention to address the gaps in responding to the needs of the sectors served by DSWD Central and Field Offices, Social Welfare and Development Agencies, Local Government Units, and other intermediaries.

WE ARE COMMITTED TO

QUALITY POLICY

We, at the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), commit to:

D

Deliver, coordinate, and monitor

Social protection programs and services to the poor, vulnerable, and disadvantaged population towards a fair, just, and peaceful society;

S

Sustain a culture of excellence

Through continual improvement of systems, mechanisms, and procedures in the delivery of programs and services;

W

Work with integrity

And adhere to ethical standards for customer satisfaction and quality service by complying with the DSWD mandates, and other pertinent laws; and

D

Demonstrate genuine concern

For the poor, prompt compassionate service, and free from any form of corruption.

Quality Assurance

Our Quality Commitment

The Social Technology Bureau adheres to the highest standards of quality in all its programs, services, and operations. We are committed to continuous improvement and excellence in serving the Filipino people.

Our quality management system ensures that all processes are transparent, accountable, and responsive to the needs of our stakeholders and the communities we serve.

We continuously review and improve our systems, guided by our commitment to deliver the best social technology programs and services for every Filipino.
