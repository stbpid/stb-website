About the Program

What is the ProtecTEEN Program?

The ProtecTEEN Program is a social welfare model fundamentally built upon a social contract with adolescent mothers and their families. This agreement outlines a mutual commitment between the program and its beneficiaries.

The program aims to protect their rights and promote their holistic well-being through comprehensive support, while beneficiaries commit to taking proactive steps for their long-term empowerment.

Who Can Avail

Beneficiary Criteria

  • Adolescent mothers aged 9–19;
  • With at least one (1) living child;
  • Adolescent mothers and/or their household are not listed as active beneficiaries of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps);
  • If below 18 years old, should not be engaged in marriage, informal union, or cohabitation with another person, in accordance with Republic Act No. 11596;
  • Categorized as vulnerable and disadvantaged based on the adolescent mother vulnerability assessment tool;
  • Willing to comply with program conditions;
  • With the adolescent's parental or guardian consent.
Goals

Objectives

The ProtecTEEN Program aims to:

  • Reduce repeated pregnancies among adolescent mothers through the knowledge and access to sexual and reproductive health (SRH) services;
  • Ensure access to and completion of appropriate educational and livelihood pathways for adolescent parents; and
  • Ensure optimal maternal and infant health outcomes through consistent access to health services.
Key Features

Program Components

Transformative Support Services

Covers sessions on psychosocial healing, health, education, and livelihood to influence positive behavioral change of adolescent parents.

Economic Empowerment Grant

Ensures commitment to avoid repeated pregnancies, pursue education and better health outcomes through conditional cash assistance.

ProtecTEEN Advocacy and Development

Establishes peer-led platforms with adolescent parents to foster solidarity and advocate against stigma and discrimination.

Holistic Support for Adolescent-Caring Families

Engages families of adolescent parents through sessions on parenting, mental health, adolescent development, and communication.

Multisectoral Support Pathway

Links government units (LGUs), schools, health centers, and the civil society to aid delivery of relevant services and improve referral systems.
