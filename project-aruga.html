About the Program

What is Project Aruga?

Project Aruga is a community-based project that provides disability inclusive social protection to children with disabilities and their families. Aruga also provides a Disability Support Allowance to parents based on the severity of the child's functioning limitations to help augment support for basic needs, health care, and education.

It responds to substantial unmet needs by providing comprehensive social services to develop the child's full potential.

Goals

Objectives

Project Aruga aims to:

  • Strengthen early detection and registration of children with disabilities through inter-agency collaboration;
  • Provide disability allowance to help cover basic service needs and related costs;
  • Improve access to health, education, livelihood, and social protection services through strengthened local support systems; and
  • Capacitate volunteers to deliver home care support services.
Who Can Avail

Beneficiaries

  • Children with disabilities belonging to poor and vulnerable households;
  • Identified beneficiaries subject to validation using data from the concerned National Government Agencies (NGAs);
  • Families and caregivers of poor children with disabilities.
Key Features

Program Components

Data Management and Referral Network

Strengthens inter-agency coordination to improve the identification, assessment, registration, and referral of children with disabilities (CWD) using standardized digitized systems.

Disability Support Allowance

Regularly provided to eligible children with disabilities to help meet additional costs related to health, education, nutrition, assistive devices, and other essential needs.

Community Mobilization and Support

Builds the capacity of implementers, parents, and community volunteers through learning sessions, to provide homebased care and community support services, while reducing stigma.

Auxiliary Services

Provides children with disabilities and their families their social welfare, health, education, social insurance, and livelihood support needs based on assessment and availability of services from strengthened inter-agency coordination.
